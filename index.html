The Gap

Democrats raised more than twice what Republicans did

Click a bar to rank its candidates. Use the buttons to switch the source of money.

Democrats outraised Republicans more than two to one. Click either party's bar to see its candidates ranked, and use the buttons above the chart to switch between total funds and each source of money: individual donors, PACs, party committees, and candidates' own wealth.

The breakdown shows that money did not guarantee a win. Sherrod Brown, Colin Allred, and Jon Tester were the race's three biggest fundraisers, and all three lost.

It also shows how far personal fortunes can go. David Trone spent $62.9 million of his own money, nearly all of the Democrats' self-funding in this group, and still lost his primary. Republicans Rick Scott and Eric Hovde each put in more than $20 million of their own.

Comparing sources reveals which candidates were powered by grassroots donors and which leaned on PACs or their own bank accounts.

Source

Method

Candidate totals come from the Federal Election Commission’s 2023-2024 candidate summary data, downloaded as a CSV and cleaned in Excel. Candidates were ranked by total receipts, and the top 25 were kept. Receipts are grouped into the FEC’s categories: individual contributions, PAC contributions, party committee contributions, and self-funding.
